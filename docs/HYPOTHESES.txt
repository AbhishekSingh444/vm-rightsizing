================================================================================
REVIEW 1 - SECTION 2: PROBLEM STATEMENT AND HYPOTHESIS (2 MARKS)
Risk-Quantified Virtual Machine Right-Sizing for Private Cloud Capacity
Reclamation
================================================================================

RUBRIC REQUIREMENT: restate the given problem in your own words, and convert it
into a falsifiable claim of the form "Method X reduces metric M by at least N%
compared to baseline B under condition C."

================================================================================
1. THE PROBLEM, RESTATED
================================================================================

A virtual machine in a private cloud is sized once, at request time, against a
peak the requester guesses at rather than measures. That guess is defensive by
construction: the cost of guessing low is a production incident, the cost of
guessing high is a line item nobody audits. The predictable result is a fleet
in which allocated capacity exceeds used capacity by a wide and persistent
margin.

Right-sizing shrinks allocations toward observed use. The operational objection
to it is not that the arithmetic is wrong but that it is incomplete. Shrinking
an allocation removes headroom, and headroom is what absorbs demand the
observation period did not contain. Every reclamation therefore purchases
capacity with an increase in the probability of future resource exhaustion.

Current practice never prices that purchase. A right-sizing tool reports what
it reclaims. It does not report what it risks, and the operator receiving the
recommendation has no basis on which to accept or reject it beyond intuition
about how spiky the workload feels. Two recommendations reclaiming identical
capacity may carry wildly different exposure, and nothing in the output
distinguishes them.

THE PROBLEM THIS PROJECT ADDRESSES IS THAT RIGHT-SIZING RECOMMENDATIONS ARE
UNPRICED. The savings side of the ledger is reported; the risk side is not
computed, not validated, and not presented. Without both sides there is no
principled operating point, only an aggressiveness dial with no units on it.

================================================================================
2. FORMAL SETTING
================================================================================

Let V be a set of virtual machines. For each VM i:

  a_orig(i) = (c_i, m_i)     original allocation, in vCPU and GB
  d_i(t)                     observed demand at sample time t, 5-minute
                             resolution, for CPU and memory
  W_obs                      observation window; recommenders see this and
                             nothing else
  W_eval                     evaluation window, strictly later; used only for
                             replay

A RECOMMENDER is a map R from the observation-window history to a flavour drawn
from a fixed catalog F. Write the resulting allocation a_R(i).

RECLAIMED CAPACITY over the evaluation window:

  Recl_cpu(R) = SUM over i of ( c_i - c_R(i) ) * |W_eval|      [vCPU-hours]
  Recl_mem(R) = SUM over i of ( m_i - m_R(i) ) * |W_eval|      [GB-hours]

EXHAUSTION is defined separately per resource, because the two failure modes
are not comparable:

  MEMORY EXHAUSTION - demand exceeds the allocation at any single sample in
  W_eval. Terminal: the workload is killed. One sample is enough.

  CPU EXHAUSTION - demand exceeds the allocation across at least 2 consecutive
  samples (10 minutes). Degrading rather than terminal, so a single-sample
  excursion is not counted; the throttling has to persist to matter.

A VIOLATION EVENT is a maximal contiguous run of exhausted samples. Recorded
metrics: event count, total duration, and S(R), the fraction of VMs in V
experiencing zero events under R.

BASELINES

  B0  As-provisioned. a_B0(i) = a_orig(i). Reference; reclaims nothing.
  B1  Peak provisioning. Allocation = max demand over W_obs, snapped to F.
      This is current practice.
  B2  P95 heuristic. Allocation = 95th percentile of demand over W_obs,
      snapped to F. This is the common heuristic recommendation.

PROPOSED METHOD  M(eps)

  For a target exhaustion probability eps: fit a Generalized Pareto tail to
  exceedances over a high threshold within W_obs, correct for serial dependence
  via the extremal index, and select the smallest flavour in F whose predicted
  probability of exhaustion over a window of length |W_eval| does not exceed
  eps. Sweeping eps traces the frontier.

================================================================================
3. HYPOTHESES
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H1 - RISK-MATCHED DOMINANCE (PRIMARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "At equal measured violation rate, M(eps) reclaims at least 15% more
   vCPU-hours and at least 10% more memory GB-hours than the P95 heuristic B2,
   on the Bitbrains GWA-T-12 fastStorage trace under a 21-day / 9-day
   observation-evaluation split."

Comparison is at MATCHED RISK, not matched aggressiveness. The P95 rule
produces one point (Recl(B2), Viol(B2)). H1 claims that the point on the
M(eps) frontier whose violation rate equals Viol(B2) sits at least 15% further
along the reclamation axis.

This framing is deliberate. A method that reclaims more AND violates more has
demonstrated nothing - it has only turned the dial harder, which anyone can do.
Dominance at matched risk is the only comparison that isolates whether the tail
model is doing real work.

NULL: the reclamation gap at matched violation rate is below 15% (CPU) or below
10% (memory), or is negative.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H2 - CALIBRATION (THE DISTINGUISHING CLAIM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "The predicted per-VM exhaustion probability is calibrated: across the sweep
   eps in {0.001, 0.005, 0.01, 0.05, 0.10}, the observed fraction of VMs
   experiencing at least one violation in W_eval falls within the 95% binomial
   confidence interval of eps, and expected calibration error over the sweep is
   below 0.05."

H2 is what separates this work from the risk-aware autoscaling literature in
the gap table. MagicScaler (C1) and comparable systems embed risk in a loss
function; none demonstrate that the risk number corresponds to anything
observable. An uncalibrated probability is a decoration - it looks like a
decision variable and cannot be used as one.

NULL: observed violation frequency falls outside the interval at two or more
sweep points, or ECE >= 0.05.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H3 - ZERO-RISK RECLAMATION QUANTUM (HEADLINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "At least 25% of allocated vCPU-hours and 20% of allocated memory GB-hours
   can be reclaimed relative to B0 with zero violation events observed in
   W_eval."

The plainest statement of the practical contribution and the one an operator
actually cares about: how much is free. Reported both fleet-wide and restricted
to non-idle VMs.

NULL: the largest reclamation achieving zero observed violations falls below
either threshold.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONDITION C (APPLIES TO ALL THREE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  DATASET      Bitbrains GWA-T-12 fastStorage, 1250 VMs, 5-minute sampling.
               Azure public VM trace as the replication dataset.
  SPLIT        First 21 days observation, final 9 days evaluation. Single
               split; no rolling origin in the primary result.
  INCLUSION    VMs with complete telemetry across both windows. Results
               reported twice: full fleet, and excluding VMs whose P99
               utilisation is below 5% of allocation.
  FLAVOURS     OpenStack-style catalog. vCPU in {1,2,4,8,16}, memory in
               {2,4,8,16,32,64} GB. Recommendations must snap to it.
  INFORMATION  Recommenders see W_obs only. No parameter, threshold or
               hyperparameter is tuned against W_eval.

================================================================================
4. WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THESE CLAIMS
================================================================================

Stated in advance, so that a negative result is a result rather than an
embarrassment.

  H1 IS FALSIFIED IF the M(eps) frontier does not lie above B2's point at
  matched risk.
  MOST LIKELY CAUSE: Bitbrains tails are light enough that the P95 quantile is
  already near-optimal, and GPD extrapolation buys nothing.

  H2 IS FALSIFIED IF observed violation rates diverge systematically from eps.
  MOST LIKELY CAUSE: serial correlation in 5-minute telemetry not fully
  absorbed by the extremal index correction, inflating the effective number of
  independent trials.

  H3 IS FALSIFIED IF no reclamation level achieves zero violations at the
  stated magnitude.
  MOST LIKELY CAUSE: a minority of highly bursty VMs violates at any reduction,
  dragging the fleet-wide zero-risk point toward zero.

The H2 failure mode is the one to watch. Utilisation samples five minutes apart
are strongly autocorrelated; treating a 9-day window as roughly 2600
independent draws will badly overstate exposure and push predicted
probabilities well above what is observed. If calibration fails, check this
before questioning the tail fit itself.

A FAILURE OF H3 IS NOT A FAILURE OF THE PROJECT. "Only 4% of capacity is
reclaimable at zero risk, and the operator should know that before believing a
vendor's 40% savings claim" is a publishable finding under the same thesis. It
is precisely the reporting deficiency this work exists to correct.

================================================================================
5. NUMERIC THRESHOLDS - A NOTE ON PROCEDURE
================================================================================

The specific figures (15%, 10%, 25%, 20%, ECE 0.05) are provisional.

  Step 1. Run a pilot on a random 50-VM subsample.
  Step 2. Fix the thresholds from the pilot and record them, with a timestamp,
          in the project log.
  Step 3. Run the full fleet.
  Step 4. DO NOT MOVE THE THRESHOLDS AFTERWARDS.

Step 4 is the whole point. A hypothesis adjusted after seeing the result is not
falsifiable, and a rubric awarding marks for a falsifiable claim is awarding
them for the discipline, not for the sentence.
